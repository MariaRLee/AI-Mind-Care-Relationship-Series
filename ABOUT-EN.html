AI-Mind Care: Relationship Series

This series is designed for everyday communication, emotional awareness, and relationship reflection across couple, parent–child, friendship, family, and workplace relationships.

The series is guided by Fr. Sinwee Chin, a Discalced Carmelite priest from Malaysia.

Hear the emotion. See the need. Help relationships move closer again.

AI is not here to decide who is right or wrong. It offers another way to understand one another. Each short video begins with an everyday conversation and practices translating emotion into needs that may be easier to understand.

AI-based “emotion translation” represents one possible perspective. It is not a diagnosis of an individual’s psychological state, personality, marriage quality, or mental health.

Every person and relationship context is different. Genuine understanding still depends on listening and dialogue between people.

If a relationship involves violence, threats, safety concerns, or serious psychological distress, appropriate professional assistance should be sought.
