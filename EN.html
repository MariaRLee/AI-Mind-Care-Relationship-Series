Season 1

It’s Not That We Don’t Love Each Other — We Just Haven’t Said It Right

Core Series Concept

In couple conflict, what we often hear is “blame.” Yet behind the blame may be feelings and needs that have never been clearly expressed.

AI is not here to decide who is right or wrong. Its role is to help translate emotion into language that may be easier for the other person to understand.
